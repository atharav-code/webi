Water-saving tips

Easy habits that make a big difference.

Fix leaks promptly

Even small drips can waste hundreds of liters per month.

Use less while washing

Turn off the tap while brushing teeth and use a bucket for car washing.

Choose water-wise plants

Plant native or drought-tolerant species to reduce outdoor watering needs.

Why water awareness matters

Water is essential for human health, agriculture, and the environment. This portal encourages informed decisions, better habits, and community action to ensure water stays available for future generations.

1 in 3 people worldwide lack safe drinking water.
70% of freshwater is used by agriculture.
20% of home water can be saved with efficient habits.
